Project overview

Magnetic probe coils convert a changing magnetic field into an induced voltage. Resolving small signals requires a signal-conditioning stage whose gain, noise, loading, and frequency response are understood together.

This project presents an INA849 amplifier PCB for that measurement chain. The engineering focus is differential signal amplification, practical PCB implementation, and calibration against a reference field or electrical input.

Component reference: Texas Instruments INA849, datasheet SBOS945B (Rev. B). Board gain, supply configuration, and measured performance still require the project schematic and test data.
The measurement chain

Sense. Amplify. Calibrate.

01 / MAGNETIC PROBE

Capture the induced voltage

For a uniform field normal to a coil, v = −NA · dB/dt. Coil area, turns, orientation, and cable loading affect the measured signal.

02 / INA849 STAGE

Condition the signal

Amplify the differential input while accounting for source impedance, common-mode range, gain bandwidth, input noise, and output headroom.

03 / CALIBRATION

Recover the field

Measure amplitude and phase through the complete chain. Use the calibrated transfer function to interpret the probe voltage.

Low-noise signal conditioning

Typical input-stage voltage noise: 1 nV/√Hz at 1 kHz, G=1000, RS=0 Ω. Output-stage noise and source impedance also contribute.

PCB implementation

Place RG close to pins 2–3; minimize parasitic capacitance and keep supply bypassing close to the device. Drive REF from a low-impedance source.
