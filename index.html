Project overview

Magnetic probe coils convert a changing magnetic field into an induced voltage. Resolving small signals requires a signal-conditioning stage whose gain, noise, loading, and frequency response are understood together.

This project presents an INA-based amplifier PCB for that measurement chain. The engineering focus is differential signal amplification, practical PCB implementation, and calibration against a reference field or electrical input.

Design-stage page: the exact INA part, assembled-board specifications, and measured performance remain to be documented.
The measurement chain

Sense. Amplify. Calibrate.

01 / MAGNETIC PROBE

Capture the induced voltage

For a uniform field normal to a coil, v = −NA · dB/dt. Coil area, turns, orientation, and cable loading affect the measured signal.

02 / INA STAGE

Condition the signal

Amplify the differential input while accounting for source impedance, common-mode range, gain bandwidth, input noise, and output headroom.

03 / CALIBRATION

Recover the field

Measure amplitude and phase through the complete chain. Use the calibrated transfer function to interpret the probe voltage.
